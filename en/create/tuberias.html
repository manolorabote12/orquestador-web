Turn pipes, let water flow: make this game without coding

Rotate the pipes until water reaches them all. Describe it in words and Orquestador builds it: levels tested by bots, art, music and ready for Google Play.

How it works

  1. Write your idea in one sentence.
  2. Chispa designs the levels, draws the characters and composes the music.
  3. Bots play every level before you get it.
  4. Publish it on Google Play with AdMob ads.
